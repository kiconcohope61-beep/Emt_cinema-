What the system can do

🎟️ Online Ticket Booking

Customers view movies, choose seats on a seat map, pay and receive tickets instantly.

💺 Real-time Seat Selection

See free and taken seats. Prevents double booking of the same seat.

💳 Payment Handling

Payment confirmation and automatic ticket generation after successful payment.

🎥 Movie & Screening Management

Projectionists add movies, upload posters and create show times for different halls.

👥 Staff & Duty Management

Managers add employees, assign roles and duties, and track attendance.

📊 Reports & Decision Support

Sales reports, occupancy and staff activity help managers make better decisions.
